# Fidelity levels

## VERIFIED

The scenario was reproduced on matching OS and development-tool versions, the raw capture is retained, primary documentation supports the setup, and all critical visible claims trace to evidence.

## RECONSTRUCTED

Exact reproduction is unavailable, but credible primary or contemporary evidence supports all critical visible behavior. Inferences and normalizations are documented.

## SIMULATED

At least one critical visible claim is based on historical plausibility rather than direct evidence. Simulated details must never be presented as observed fact.

Scenario fidelity is the lowest fidelity of any critical visible claim. Evidence quantity does not compensate for an unsupported critical claim.
